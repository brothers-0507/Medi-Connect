Doctor Portal Operations & Clinical Instructions Guide

Simple and easily understandable instructions on how to operate this clinician portal.

1. Fast Digital Prescriptions

Click "Create New Prescription" to launch the Creator in an overlay popup modal. Enter registered patient username for instant profile autofill, configure medications with standardized dosage/frequency selectors, or click 1-Click Clinical Presets (Fever, Antibiotics, GERD, Allergy, BP) to populate complete regimens instantly.

2. Overlay Modal Workflow

The prescription creator opens in a focused overlay modal without obstructing your workspace view. You can clear fields, apply customized presets, submit prescriptions seamlessly via AJAX, and immediately issue consecutive prescriptions for waiting patients without full page refreshes.

3. Real-Time Patient Push & QR

Upon submission, prescriptions are pushed immediately to the recipient patient's portal with an audible chime and real-time alert banner. Patients can access their prescription, import doses directly into their Medi-Tracker adherence schedule, or broadcast for pharmacy quotes.

4. Patients History & Time Audit

Every issued prescription is archived with the verified date and accurate time of issuance (e.g., 11 Oct 2026, 08:42 PM). Filter between All, Pending Claim, and Dispensed at Pharmacy with real-time text search across patient names, codes, and medicines.

5. Privacy-Preserving Broadcasting

Full clinical records and notes are accessible strictly by doctor and patient. When patients broadcast for pharmacy quotes or use Quick Checkout, only prescribed medicines and quantities are shared. Doctor clinical notes and confidential contact records remain completely private.

6. Credentials & Badges

Maintain verified credentials including your Medical Registration Number, Department Specialty, and Healthcare Facility Affiliation. Credentials sync to your banner and all officially generated prescription sheets automatically.
