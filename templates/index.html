How MediConnect Protects & Empowers You

Private Relations

Doctor-patient connections are isolated. Doctors lookup patients solely by their secure registered username, preventing public user registry leaks.

Popup Automation

No manual data entry. Prescriptions pop up directly on the patient's dashboard, importing dosages and schedules automatically into their tracker on acceptance.

FIFO Expiry Alarms

Pharmacy checkouts deduct oldest stock first (FIFO) and trigger real-time near-expiry warnings to prevent dispensing decaying stock.
